Alien is installed

  1. Start Rhino 8 and type Grasshopper.
  2. Find Alien on the Alien tab. Place a node, then click PANEL on it to open the Alien panel.
  3. In the panel, click ? (Help). It shows how to make an OpenRouter account and link your key, step by step.

Your scripts are kept in Documents/Alien scripts. To remove Alien, quit Rhino and delete Alien.gha from Grasshopper's Libraries folder (in Grasshopper: File > Special Folders > Components Folder).
