Alien for Grasshopper

Alien adds nodes to Grasshopper that write their own script from a plain-English description, plus a suite of architectural nodes: Victorian site maps, drawing views, levels, grids, walls, doors and windows, curtain walls, beams, columns and connections.

  • It needs Rhino 8. Please quit Rhino before installing.
  • It installs for your user account only: Alien goes into Grasshopper's Libraries folder in your Library.
  • Nothing else on your Mac is changed, and no administrator password is needed.

Describing nodes and the AI nodes use OpenRouter, paid from your own credit (about US$0.002 a node). After installing, the Alien panel's Help walks you through connecting it.
